Frame rate check

Lab 4 test page. It answers two questions for this phone: (1) what a slow-motion or 60 fps video looks like when a web page opens it, and (2) how fast the in-browser camera can run. Nothing is uploaded. Everything stays on the phone.

1. Check a video you already filmed

  1. In the normal Camera app, film about 5 seconds of something moving (a hand wave is fine). Do it once in Slo-mo (iPhone) or Slow motion (Galaxy). If you can, also film once in normal video at 60 fps.
  2. Come back here and pick the video.






  
2. Check the in-browser camera

  
The browser will ask for camera permission. It asks the back camera for 240, 120, 60 and 30 fps and reports what it actually gets.

  
  
  






  
3. Send the results

  
Copy and paste into a message, or take a screenshot of this page.
