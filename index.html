Flow Patterns: three ways to see a flow

Streamlines, pathlines, and streaklines are three different ways to draw the same flow. In this lab, you draw all three lines yourself in two situations: steady flow and unsteady flow.

Three lines

StreamlineAt one instant, a line that follows the direction the water is moving
PathlineThe actual path one particle travels
StreaklineA line joining all particles that passed through one point

Two situations

Steady flowNo obstruction: the flow stays the same over time
Unsteady flowWith obstruction: the flow keeps changing behind the obstruction
